TRADER TOM MECHANICAL ENTRIES

Espresso

Bracket order of the 09:29 1-minute bar.

  • Valid for the first 3 minutes.
  • Cancel if not triggered after 3 minutes.
  • Use a buffer.
  • Purpose: scalp early momentum.
  • Can hold if momentum or trend continuation looks likely.

School Run

Buy the break of BAR 2 on the 15-minute chart.

Anti Trade

Taking the opposite side of the traditional School Run.

  • Used when the School Run bar is within the overnight range.
  • If closing above the overnight range, look to buy below the School Run bar.
  • Inverse logic applies for shorts.

Flip the Switch

Taking the other side of the School Run trade if the initial entry fails.

Discretion

If trader instinct kicks in, I can choose to buy or sell the high or low of the School Run bar based on discretion.

Management

Pyramid

  • Add to winners.
  • Never add to losers.
  • Use larger order initially, then smaller scale-ins.
  • Example: initial entry 30, scale in 10, then scale in 15.

Profit Taking

  • I do not have a fixed take profit.
  • I read the market to the best of my ability and accept the outcome.
  • Scale out of winners.

Stops

  • Stops are NEVER tight.

Targets

  • Measured moves of the 15-minute bar are a way to establish a reasonable target.

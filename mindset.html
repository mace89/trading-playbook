MINDSET RULES

Pre-Trade Checklist

  • Am I calm?
  • Am I using the correct contract size?
  • Is this trade within my $500 daily risk limit?
  • Can I hold this trade for at least 15 minutes?
  • Am I entering because of a valid setup, not because I am trying to win money back?

Core Reflection

  • I get too confident when the account is in profit, especially around +$1,000.
  • I get tilted when I make a mistake, such as entering with more contracts than intended.
  • If I enter with too much size, I must fix it immediately by exiting the position.
  • I have a tendency to risk too heavily and too aggressively.
  • Going from +$1,200 to -$2,000 on the day is not acceptable.

Main Mindset Rule

I need to become very humble.

My job is not just to make money. My job is to control risk, protect the account, and avoid big red days.

Daily Drawdown Rule

  • Maximum daily drawdown: $500.
  • This allows enough room to trade NQ and YM with possible scale-ins.
  • If I risk $200 on NQ and $200 on YM, I only have $100 left for any scale-in.
  • Once the risk budget is used, I stop adding risk.

Position Holding Rule

  • Do not enter a trade if I cannot hold it for at least 15 minutes.
  • I get triggered when I go long, price looks weak mid-candle, I close and flip short, then the candle closes bullish and goes in my original direction.
  • I must wait for candle closes and avoid reacting emotionally mid-candle.

Tom Hougaard Principle

I know I can make money using Tom Hougaard-style methods.

My edge is not the problem. My risk control and emotions is what I need conquer on a daily basis.

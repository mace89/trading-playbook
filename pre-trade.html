🧠 PRE-TRADE RESET

Before you trade, you reset your state.
Not your strategy — your behaviour.

🎯 MANTRAS (SAY THESE SLOWLY)

  • I am not here to fix what just happened
  • I do not trade to escape frustration
  • If I feel urgency, I am already wrong
  • Missing a trade is better than forcing one
  • My job is to wait, not to act
  • I do not act while emotionally activated

🧠 HUMILITY TRUTH

I am not in control of outcomes.
I am only in control of execution.

The market does not care what I think should happen.

🔥 YOUR FAILURE MODE

You do NOT blow accounts from bad strategy.
You blow accounts when you try to fix emotional discomfort.

  • Frustration → urgency
  • Loss → revenge trading
  • Confusion → forced trades

👉 If you notice this, you are no longer trading your system.

🧘 RESET RULE

  • No urgency = allowed to trade
  • Any emotional activation = no trading
  • Wait until calm returns

CALM → PATIENCE → EXECUTION

Nothing else matters in the session.

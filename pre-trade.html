🧠 PRE-TRADE RESET

You reset your ego before you trade.

🎯 MANTRAS (READ SLOWLY)

  • I am not here to fix what just happened
  • I do not trade to escape frustration
  • If I feel urgency, I am already wrong
  • Missing a trade is better than forcing one
  • My job is to wait, not to act
  • I do not act while emotionally activated

🧠 POST-WIN HUMILITY (IMPORTANT FOR YOU)

  • Winning does not mean I am doing anything special
  • Profit does not mean I am in control
  • A win does not change the next trade
  • I am not owed continued success
  • Confidence is not edge
  • One loss can remove recent gains

👉 The moment I expect to keep profits, I lose objectivity.

🧠 HUMILITY TRUTH

I am not in control of outcomes.
I am only in control of execution.

The market does not care about my recent results.

🔥 YOUR REAL FAILURE LOOP

Ego does not come from losses — it comes from recent wins.

  • Wins → overconfidence
  • Overconfidence → expectation
  • Expectation → emotional reaction to loss
  • Reaction → spiral

🧘 RESET RULE

  • No urgency = allowed to trade
  • Any emotional activation = no trading
  • Wins do NOT change behaviour

CALM → DISCIPLINE → EXECUTION

Every trade is independent of the last.
